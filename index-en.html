Philosophy of Care

Clinical precision. Timely decisions. Extraordinary care.

I believe that when a medical situation is complex, every decision takes on extraordinary value.

My philosophy of care starts from a fundamental premise: high-specialty medicine must combine scientific excellence, clinical experience, medical judgment, and deeply human care.

I don't simply aim to treat an illness. I aim to understand the person as a whole, identify what truly matters, recognize risks that might otherwise go unnoticed, and establish a clear clinical course.

1. Clinical excellence

Every patient deserves a rigorous, individualized assessment grounded in the best available scientific evidence.

I combine specialized knowledge, clinical experience, and critical thinking to analyze even the most complex scenarios. Excellence isn't about doing more — it's about doing what's right.

2. Precision in every decision

In critical situations, uncertainty can have serious consequences.

That's why my approach focuses on distinguishing the urgent from the important, identifying risks, questioning assumptions, and making well-founded decisions. Every intervention must have a reason. Every decision must have a purpose.

3. Timeliness

In emergency and resuscitation medicine, time is a clinical factor.

I know when to observe, when to investigate, when to intervene, and when to escalate care. Acting at the right time is also a form of precision.

4. Personalized care

Every patient is different.

Their history, context, risks, expectations, and goals are all part of the medical decision. That's why I avoid generic solutions and build individualized recommendations that respond to each person's actual clinical reality.

5. Safety

Safety begins before any intervention.

I identify risks, anticipate scenarios of deterioration, and work to reduce both the risk of omission and the risk of unnecessary interventions. Sophisticated medicine isn't the kind that intervenes more — it's the kind that knows when to intervene and when not to.

6. Second opinions with independent judgment

A second opinion should offer more than just confirmation.

It should allow for a review of the diagnosis, interpretation of the available evidence, identification of alternatives, acknowledgment of uncertainties, and understanding of the possible consequences of each path. My purpose is to provide clarity for decision-making, always respecting the patient's autonomy and their relationship with their treating medical team.

7. High-level communication

Medical complexity should never become a barrier for the patient.

I explain the problem, the alternatives, the risks, and the uncertainties clearly and understandably. Because a truly informed decision requires understanding — not just receiving information.

8. Humanity in critical moments

High specialization is not at odds with closeness.

I recognize that behind every diagnosis there is a person, a family, and often a moment of deep uncertainty. That's why my care seeks to combine clinical firmness with human sensitivity.

9. Confidentiality and trust

Trust is an essential part of medical practice.

I protect patient privacy, respect confidentiality, and strive to ensure that every interaction takes place within a professional, discreet, and secure environment.

10. Continuity of medical judgment

Exceptional care doesn't end when the consultation does.

I make sure the patient understands what was found, what it means, what needs to be monitored, and what the next steps are. My goal isn't just to resolve a consultation — it's to bring clarity to the decision-making process.

FAQ frecuentes

How do I book an appointment?

The fastest way is to write on WhatsApp with your name and a brief description of your reason for consultation. I will reply as soon as possible, always prioritizing urgent cases. You can also book directly on my Calendly calendar, choosing the time that works best for you.

What should I bring to my first consultation?

An official ID, your medical card or a summary of previous studies if you have them (lab work, imaging, notes from other physicians), and a list of any medications you currently take, if applicable.

Can I call directly instead of writing on WhatsApp?

Yes, the call button is available in the top menu of the page. However, WhatsApp usually gets a faster response, especially if I'm attending to a patient.

Can the consultation be done by video call?

Yes. The telemedicine option is designed for guidance and follow-up when travel is not feasible — see the full details in the "Emergency Care" card above. For procedures or a physical exam, the consultation must be in person.

Do you see emergencies outside your regular hours?

Yes. For relative urgency cases I can see patients outside the published hours, with prior confirmation by WhatsApp. If it is a life-threatening emergency, go immediately to the nearest hospital emergency department.

Do you accept medical insurance, or is it private consultation only?

Currently, consultations are private. If your case requires hospital management, I can guide you through the referral process according to your insurer or institution.

What should I do if my case requires hospitalization or an urgent transfer?

If we determine during the consultation that you need hospitalization, I'll guide you through the admission process and can coordinate an ambulance transfer if needed. I have hospital affiliation with Hospital H+ Querétaro or the hospital of your preference to ensure continuity of care.

Do you see patients in other cities, or only in Querétaro?

In-person consultations and the ambulance service are available in Querétaro. However, the telemedicine option lets me provide guidance, follow-up, or a second opinion no matter what city you're in.
